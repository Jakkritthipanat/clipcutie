Short product videos, made with AI and posted to TikTok

ClipCutie turns a product listing into a 15–30 second vertical video with a script, scenes, captions and hashtags, then posts it to the creator's own TikTok account after they sign in with TikTok.

How it works

✍️ AI script and captions

Writes a short hook, scene-by-scene script, caption and hashtags from the product's name, photos and details.

🎬 Vertical video, ready to post

Builds a 9:16 clip with an attention hook in the first seconds and a clear call to action at the end.

🎵 Post to your TikTok

After you sign in with TikTok, you review the caption and privacy setting, then ClipCutie posts the video to your account.

🏷️ Honest labels

Videos are labelled as AI-generated content, and you can disclose posts that promote products as commercial content before posting.

How it works

  1. Add products to the ClipCutie queue (product name, photos, price and your own product link).
  2. ClipCutie writes the script and makes a short vertical video. The video and its caption are saved on your computer.
  3. Click Connect TikTok and sign in with TikTok (TikTok Login Kit). You choose which permissions to grant.
  4. Before each post you see the video, caption, hashtags, who can view it and the interaction settings, and you confirm it.
  5. ClipCutie sends the video to TikTok with the Content Posting API and shows you when TikTok has finished processing it.

Who it's for

Independent online sellers and affiliate creators who post product videos to their own TikTok accounts. ClipCutie runs on the user's own computer as a browser extension with a small desktop helper. It does not run its own servers and does not store your TikTok data anywhere else.
