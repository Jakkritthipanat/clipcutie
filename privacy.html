Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information ClipCutie ("we", "the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. ClipCutie is a browser extension with a small desktop helper that runs on your own computer. We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal data.

1. Information we receive from TikTok

When you choose Connect TikTok and approve access through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok gives ClipCutie:

  • Basic profile information: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), used to show which account you are posting to.
  • An access token and refresh token, used to upload and publish the videos you choose (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • Creator settings returned by TikTok before posting, such as the privacy options available to your account and the maximum video length, used only to show the posting options to you.
  • The processing status of videos you post, used to tell you whether a post succeeded.

We do not read your followers, messages, watch history, or other people's content.

2. Content you create

Product details you add (name, photos, price, your product links), the scripts, captions and videos ClipCutie makes, and your settings are stored in your browser's extension storage and in folders you choose on your computer.

3. How we use information

  • To make videos and captions for the products you add.
  • To post a video to your TikTok account only after you review it and confirm the post.
  • To show you posting status and errors.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling, and we never post without your confirmation.

4. Sharing

  • TikTok receives the video, caption and settings you choose when you post.
  • AI services you connect (for example Google Gemini with your own API key) receive the product details and prompts needed to write scripts and captions. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Facebook receives a video only if you separately connect a Facebook Page and choose to post there.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share TikTok user data with any other third party.

5. Storage and security

TikTok tokens and profile data are stored only on your computer, in the extension's local storage, and are sent only to TikTok's servers over HTTPS. Anyone with access to your computer account could access them, so keep your device secured.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Click Disconnect TikTok in ClipCutie to delete the stored tokens and profile data immediately.
  • You can also revoke ClipCutie's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy › Security and permissions › Apps and services permissions.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything stored in its local storage. Videos and files saved to your folders stay until you delete them.

7. Children

ClipCutie is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly handle children's data.

8. Your rights

Because your data stays on your computer, you can view, change or delete it yourself at any time. For any privacy question or request, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be shown in the app.
